About the Bhagavad Gita

A conversation that has inspired millions.

The Bhagavad Gita is a sacred dialogue between Lord Krishna and the warrior Arjuna on the battlefield of Kurukshetra.

Through their conversation, Krishna explains the nature of duty, action, knowledge, devotion, self-control and the path toward spiritual realization.

Its teachings continue to inspire people across generations because they address universal questions about life, purpose, responsibility and inner peace.
